Le Club Chasse et Pêche

Old Montréal$$$$Michelinclosed Sun/Mon

The big-ticket dinner — seasonal Québécois, intimate, oysters to die for. A top MTLàTABLE booking. Reserve as early as you can.

Barroco

Old Montréal$$$French · romantic

Candlelit, rustic, perfect first-night dinner steps from the old-town hotels. Filet and braised beef are the moves.

Garde Manger

Old Montréal$$$$seafood · lively

Chuck Hughes' buzzy seafood spot — go for the tasting menu and the energy. Great backup to Barroco.

Le Majestique

Plateau · The Main$$small plates

Eclectic, fun, a little chaotic — order family-style. Oysters, tartare, and a late bar vibe. Very your-speed.

Bistrot La Fabrique

Plateau · St-Denis$$bistro · date night

Cozy open-kitchen bistro, famous french toast, thoughtful wine. The sweet-spot date dinner.

Schwartz's Deli

The Main$$institution

The mandatory Montréal smoked-meat sandwich since 1928. Cramped, shared tables, cash-friendly — go off-peak.

La Banquise

Plateau$poutine · 24h

30+ poutines, open all night — the perfect drunk-o'clock or hungover 2am plan. Try L'Obélix (smoked meat).

Fairmount & St-Viateur Bagel

Mile End$the showdown

Two legendary wood-fired bagel shops, two blocks apart, both ~24h. Buy from both, decide who's right.

Crew Collective & Café

Old Montréal$$the photo

Coffee and brunch inside a jaw-dropping former bank hall — soaring vaulted ceilings. Get the grilled cheese.

Chez José Café

Plateau$cozy · brunchclosed Wed

Tiny, warm, huge personality — breakfast sandwiches, pastéis de nata, Portuguese leanings. Weekend waitlist.

L'Avenue

Plateau$$the blowout

Legendary over-the-top brunch — giant plates, pistachio french toast. Expect a line; worth it.

Café Olimpico

Mile End$espresso

Old-school Italian espresso bar. Cappuccino + cannoli, no rush, all the neighborhood energy.

The Coldroom

Old Montréal$$$speakeasy

Hidden basement bar behind an unmarked door — ring the bell. Excellent, strong cocktails; great first-night move.

Bar Bisou Bisou

Old Montréal$$sexy · low-keyclosed Mon

Sultry basement cocktail bar, exposed brick, low-ABV menu that tells you exactly how each drink tastes.

Cloakroom Bar

Downtown$$$bespoke

No menu — tell them what you like, they build it. Hidden behind a tailor shop. Small; go early or expect a wait.

Terrasse Nelligan

Old Montréal · rooftop$$$seasonal — call

Best-known rooftop with old-port views. Beautiful when warm — in November it may be closed or heated-only, so check first.
