Where to stay

Base in the
old town.

Stay in Old Montréal and your whole first night — and half the weekend — is on foot. All three below have parking sorted, which matters since you're driving.

The Bresoles Hotel

Old Montréal$$–$$$our pick

New, spacious studio-style rooms with kitchenettes, right by Notre-Dame and steps from the bars and restaurants. Has its own parking garage (~$50/night) — the easy button for a road trip.

Best all-rounder: space, location, parking, value.

Auberge du Vieux-Port

Old Port waterfront$$$vibe · history

Handsome historic building right on the waterfront, cozy bar downstairs, cafés and restaurants within a one-minute walk. The most "we're on a trip" feeling of the three.

Best for atmosphere and a great location.

Arcadia Hôtel Boutique

Across from the Old Port$$apartment-style

Boutique apartment-style rooms — washer/dryer, coffee machine, robes — across from the water. Self check-in via app (heads up: a small insurance/deposit fee, no full-time front desk).

Best if you want an apartment feel over a hotel.

Booking a couple's weekend. If you want to splurge, Hôtel William Gray and Hôtel Nelligan are the design-forward, rooftop-having upgrades in the same neighborhood. Book any of these 3–6 weeks out for early November.
Parking reality. Old Montréal street parking is scarce and metered. Book a hotel with a garage, or use an app like ClicknPark to reserve a nearby lot in advance. Then leave the car parked all weekend — you won't need it in the city.
