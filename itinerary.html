Arrive & Old Montréal

◐ leave early · ~6h drive + border · dark by ~4:40pm
morning
Drive

Hit the road

Aim to leave by 8–9am so you clear the border in daylight. Coffee, playlists, passports in the glovebox. Route & border →

~3:30pm
Check in

Drop bags in Old Montréal

Base yourselves in the old town so everything tonight is on foot. Hotel picks →

4:30pm
See

Old Port at dusk

Walk Rue Saint-Paul's cobblestones down to the waterfront while the light goes. Cold and windy off the river — duck into shops when you need to warm up.

7:30pm
Dinner

Barroco

Rustic, romantic, candlelit French right in the old town — a perfect first night. $$$ Book ahead. Backup: Garde Manger.

10pm
Nightcap

The Coldroom

Speakeasy behind an unmarked door — ring the bell, head to the basement. Two minutes from your hotel. cocktails

Day 02 · Saturday

Old town, downtown & a night out

◑ the big day — pace the caffeine
10am
Brunch

Crew Collective & Café

Brunch inside a cathedral-like former bank hall — soaring ceilings, gorgeous, very worth the photo. Grilled cheese + great coffee.

12pm
See

Notre-Dame Basilica

The cobalt-and-gold interior is the single best indoor sight in the city. ~$16. If you love it, the evening AURA light show is unreal.

2pm
Do

Museum of Fine Arts or The Main

Rainy? The MMFA is huge and warm. Rather browse? Wander Boulevard Saint-Laurent for shops, murals and vintage.

7:30pm
Dinner

Le Majestique

Loud, fun, oysters-and-small-plates in the Plateau — order lots, share everything. $$

10pm
Late

Poutine + a bar

Cheese-curd emergency at La Banquise (open 24h), then cocktails at Bar Bisou Bisou. Or save Le Club Chasse et Pêche for tonight — it's the Michelin one, closed Sun/Mon.

Day 03 · Sunday

Bagels, Mile End & the mountain

◒ shoot the skyline at midday
9:30am
Eat

The bagel showdown

Buy a warm sesame at Fairmount AND at St-Viateur two blocks over. Settle the debate yourselves.

10:15am
Café

Café Olimpico

Old-school Italian espresso bar, the heart of Mile End. Cappuccino + cannoli, watch the street.

12pm
See

Jean-Talon Market

Big (partly indoor) market in Little Italy — cheese, cider, oysters. Graze your way through for lunch.

2:30pm
See

Mount Royal — Kondiaronk lookout

The city's best skyline view. Do it in daylight (sunset ~4:40). Walk up, or grab the #11 bus if it's icy. Warm up in the chalet.

7:30pm
Dinner

Bistrot La Fabrique

Intimate Plateau bistro famous for its french toast — great date-night dinner. $$

10pm
Nightcap

Cloakroom Bar

No menu — they build a cocktail to your taste. Hidden behind a tailor shop downtown.

Day 04 · Monday

Slow morning & the drive home

◓ check out, then point the car south
10am
Brunch

Chez José Café

Cozy, characterful Plateau brunch (Portuguese-leaning). Or go big at L'Avenue if you want the classic blowout.

12pm
Last stop

Atwater Market

Pretty indoor market on the canal — grab cheese, pastries and snacks for the road, then a quick stroll through Little Burgundy.

1:30pm
Drive

Head home

~6h + border. Leaving early afternoon gets you back at a decent hour. Declare anything you bought.

Weather & ice. Your biggest risk isn't crime — it's slipping. Sidewalks glaze over. Grippy shoes, penguin steps, and the Underground City (RÉSO) when it's nasty.
