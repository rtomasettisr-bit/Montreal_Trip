Day 02 · Saturday

Old town, downtown & a night out

◑ the big day — pace the caffeine
10am
Brunch

Crew Collective & Café

Brunch inside a jaw-dropping former bank hall — soaring ceilings, very worth the photo. Grilled cheese + great coffee.

12pm
See

Notre-Dame Basilica

The cobalt-and-gold interior is the best indoor sight in town. ~$16 CAD. Love it? The evening AURA light show is unreal.

2pm
Do

Bota Bota — spa on the water

Thermal circuit (sauna → cold plunge → soak) on a boat with old-port views. The perfect couple's antidote to a cold day. Book the circuit ahead.

7:30pm
Dinner

Le Majestique

Loud, fun, oysters-and-small-plates in the Plateau — order lots, share everything. $$

10pm+
Late

Poutine + a bar

Cheese-curd emergency at La Banquise (open 24h), cocktails at Bar Bisou Bisou. Or make tonight the Le Club Chasse et Pêche splurge (closed Sun/Mon).

Day 03 · Sunday

Bagels, Mile End & the mountain

◒ shoot the skyline at midday
9:30am
Eat

The bagel showdown

Warm sesame at Fairmount AND at St-Viateur two blocks over. Settle it yourselves.

10:15am
Café

Café Olimpico

Old-school Italian espresso bar, the heart of Mile End. Cappuccino + cannoli, watch the street.

12pm
See

Jean-Talon Market

Big (partly indoor) Little Italy market — cheese, cider, oysters. Graze your way through for lunch.

2:30pm
See

Mount Royal — Kondiaronk lookout

The city's best skyline view. Do it in daylight (sunset ~4:40). Walk up or grab the #11 bus if it's icy.

7:30pm
Dinner

Bistrot La Fabrique

Intimate Plateau bistro, famous french toast — a great date-night dinner. $$

10pm
Nightcap

Cloakroom Bar

No menu — they build a cocktail to your taste. Hidden behind a tailor shop.

Day 04 · Monday

Slow morning & the drive home

◓ check out, point the car south
10am
Brunch

Chez José Café

Cozy, characterful Plateau brunch. Or go big at L'Avenue for the classic blowout.

12pm
Last stop

Atwater Market

Pretty indoor market on the canal — snacks for the road, quick stroll through Little Burgundy.

1:30pm
Drive

Head home

~6h + border. Leaving early afternoon gets you back at a decent hour. Declare anything you bought.
